MAIN MENU ITEM 2 — INSTITUTIONAL INFORMATION

2.1 History & Background
In 1999, a negotiation was initiated between Christian Children's Fund of Canada (CCFC) and the Ghana Baptist Convention (GBC) to establish a partnership for implementing child sponsorship activities. Several conditions were set for this collaboration.
The Northern Ghana Baptist Associations of GBC (Tamale, Liberty, Nalerigu, Nankpanduri and Bolgatanga associations) were invited to participate. First Baptist Church, under the leadership of Rev. Stephen Abu Mahama, accepted the request and paid the required GHC 500 commitment fee on the condition that the project — Baptist Child Development Project (BCDP) — be located in the Tolon-Kumbungu District, their Evangelism and Missions field.
The program operates under the registration of Baptist Relief and Development Agency (BREDA), the development wing of the Ghana Baptist Convention, as its affiliate. For the first five years, all project staff were employed from the Baptist family; in 2005, the leadership broadened staffing to include all denominations.
2.2 Profile / Overview
BREDA – Baptist Child Development Programs (CDP) is the child-focused development arm of the Ghana Baptist Convention, headquartered in Nyankpala, Northern Ghana. The organization implements community-based programs targeting vulnerable children and families in deprived communities across the Northern Region. Operating since 2000 under partnership with Children Believe (formerly CCFC) and the Korea Fund, BREDA-CDP runs two main programs — BCDP (0504) and TBCDP (0509) — serving over 2,600 registered children and their families.
2.3 Mission Statement
Baptist Child Development Project works in partnership with deprived Communities and other partners using appropriate interventions to meet expressed needs in Ghana, with particular attention to the North where abject poverty is prevalent.


2.4 Vision Statement

ABOUT PAGE

Who We Are
BREDA – Baptist Child Development Programs is a faith-based non-governmental organization and the development arm of the Ghana Baptist Convention. Established in 2000 through a partnership with Christian Children's Fund of Canada (CCFC), we work with deprived communities across the Northern Region of Ghana to improve the quality of life for vulnerable children and their families.
Our History
BREDA-CDP was born out of a 1999 negotiation between CCFC and the Ghana Baptist Convention. First Baptist Church, under Rev. Stephen Abu Mahama, championed the initiative and ensured the project was anchored in the Tolon-Kumbungu District — the church's primary missions field. Over two decades, we have grown to serve thousands of children across multiple programs and communities.
Our Mission
To work in partnership with deprived Communities and other stakeholders, using appropriate interventions to meet expressed needs in Ghana — with particular focus on the North where abject poverty is prevalent.
Our Vision
Communities with empowered families living to their full potentials — socially, economically, and spiritually.
Our Core Values
•	Integrity — We do what we say
•	Transparency — Open in all our dealings
•	Accountability — Answerable to communities, partners, and God
•	Christ-Centred Service — Motivated by faith and compassion
•	Child-Focused — Every decision centers on child wellbeing
•	Respect for All — Dignity for every person we serve
Our Governance
BREDA-CDP is governed by a 7-member Board of Directors drawn from fields of Community Development, Health, Monitoring & Evaluation, and Education. The Board ensures strong policy direction, financial accountability, and alignment with the organization's mission.
 
PROGRAMS / SERVICES PAGE

Our Development Programs
1. Education
We believe education is the foundation of change. Our education interventions support children, teachers, and school systems in partner communities.
•	Infrastructure development: classroom blocks, resource centres, ECD playgrounds
•	Support for volunteer teachers in continuing education
•	Annual provision of school materials to all sponsored children
•	School uniform distribution
•	Recreational and exposure trips for children
2. Health & Nutrition
Healthy children learn better and grow stronger. Our health programs address both preventive and curative care.
•	School feeding programs in partner communities
•	Medical assistance for sponsored children
•	HIV/AIDS education and nutritional support
•	District-wide de-worming (twice per year)
•	Malaria control and ITN distribution
•	CHPS compound renovation for improved community healthcare
3. Water, Sanitation & Hygiene (WASH)
Access to clean water and sanitation is a right. BREDA-CDP has invested significantly in WASH infrastructure across our partner communities.
•	23 Rainwater Harvesting Tanks installed in 15 schools
•	Borehole drilling and mechanization
•	400+ soak-away units constructed
•	School urinal pits and household toilets
•	Monthly community clean-up exercises
•	WASH sensitization and behavior change communication
4. Sustainable Livelihood Development
Economic empowerment builds resilience. Our livelihoods programs equip community members — especially women — to generate and manage income sustainably.
•	Village Savings and Loan Associations (VSLAs) — 81 functional groups
•	Youth Savings and Loan Associations (YSLAs) — 46 active groups
•	Micro-credit disbursement and management training
•	Small business development support
